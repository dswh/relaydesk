# Resolve urgent support issues

SignalDesk treats security incidents, payment failures, outages, and account takeovers as urgent. Provide a clear subject and a short description. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or payment card data.

## Frequently asked questions

### How fast are urgent tickets reviewed?

The response target is 60 minutes.

### What information should I include?

Include the affected feature and a safe description of the problem. Never include secrets.
