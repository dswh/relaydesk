Open trace review

We have a help assistant and a growing set of customer questions.
We want every answer to pass a fixed check.
When a question fails, the loop should make one small correction and measure again.

Semantic scoring pending

Loading the current loop state.

The desired state: every question passes all three repetitions, with zero critical failures.
The total score is 100 times passing questions divided by total questions.
Each question gets one vote. Its labels explain why it failed.

The arithmetic is deterministic. Answer meaning needs human judgment or a judge validated against human labels. A fixture or an unscored answer cannot establish quality.
